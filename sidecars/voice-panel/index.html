Info & User Guide

How It Works

How It Works

Antigravity Voice TTS (v2.1.0) connects to Antigravity's turn lifecycle via a background Stop hook. When an assistant finishes a response, the smart output pipeline parses Markdown blocks (tables, headings, nested lists, code, diagrams, callouts, and LaTeX math), normalizes hard symbols for smooth speech, and streams balanced segments to this sidecar panel.

Reading Filters & Dual View

  • Readable vs. Spoken View: Shows clean Readable text with original symbols by default while speaking smooth normalized phrasing. Switch to Spoken anytime to preview the exact voice script.
  • Reading Profiles: Pick Balanced (reads small tables, summarizes large ones), Room Listen (reads all tables, structure & paths hands-free), Prose Only (skips tables, code & diagrams), or Minimal—or fine-tune each type & custom pronunciation rules in Advanced.
  • On-Demand Expand & Instant Re-apply: Click Read rows / Read lines ▶ on any summarized block to hear it in full, click ↻ Re-apply to update the active transcript right away after changing filters, or use ↻ in the top bar to reload the panel.

Using It in Conversations

  • Auto-Speak Live Responses: Keep Auto-speak assistant turns enabled in Reading Filters & Automation to automatically hear new chat responses as soon as the agent finishes a turn.
  • Interactive Segmented Reader: Below the spectrum visualizer, every sentence is numbered and highlighted as it plays. Click any segment in the reader to jump directly to that sentence.
  • Transport & Repeat Controls: Use Back and Next to step between sentences, Play / Pause to pause mid-sentence, or cycle Repeat between Off, Repeat: 1 (loop current sentence), and Repeat: All (loop entire response).
  • Listen to Past Conversations & Artifacts: In Conversation Turns, the active chat is automatically detected (★ [Current Chat]), or you can pick any past conversation from the dropdown. Click Load on any turn or Markdown artifact (📄 *.md) to inspect its segments first, or click ▶ to listen immediately.

Engines & Memory Management

  • Kokoro-82M (WebGPU / Neural): Runs an 82M-parameter ONNX neural voice model locally on your GPU (with automatic WASM fallback). Model weights are cached on disk after the first load.
  • Auto-Unload & Manual Unload: To save VRAM/RAM, Kokoro-82M automatically unloads after inactivity (default 10 min, adjustable) or when you click Unload, and automatically reloads from disk cache in ~1–2s when needed again.
  • Collapsible Workspace: Click any window title bar (or use Compact View at the top) to retract settings windows down to their title bars—your layout is saved automatically.
